01

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files placed on a browser or device. Websites may also use local storage, session storage and similar technologies to remember information or support site functions. This policy uses cookies as a general term for these technologies where appropriate.

02

How This Site Uses Storage

The current website uses limited session storage to remember when the Sunday Reservation invitation has been dismissed. That preference lasts for the browsing session and helps prevent the invitation from appearing repeatedly.

Our hosting and form providers may use essential technologies needed to deliver the website, process submissions, prevent spam and maintain security.

03

Essential Technologies

Essential technologies support navigation, forms, security and basic preferences. The site may not work as intended if they are blocked. These technologies are used for operation rather than advertising.

04

Analytics And Advertising

Sunday & Company does not currently use optional advertising cookies on this website. If analytics or advertising tools are introduced, this policy will be updated and any notice or consent choice required by applicable law will be provided.

05

Form Processing

Forms are processed through Web3Forms. The provider may use limited technical information and anti-spam measures when a form is submitted. Review the provider’s policies for details about its own technology and processing practices.

06

Third-Party Websites

Links to social platforms and other outside websites may lead to services that use their own cookies. Sunday does not control cookies placed after you leave this website. Your choices on those services are governed by their policies and settings.

07

Your Browser Controls

Most browsers allow you to delete stored data, block cookies or receive a notice before a cookie is placed. Private browsing modes may also limit how long information remains on your device.

Blocking essential storage may affect form behavior or cause preferences to reset. The controls available to you depend on the browser and device you use.

08

How Long Information Remains

The newsletter invitation preference is stored for the current session. Other essential technologies used by hosting or form providers may follow the durations set by those providers. Personal information submitted through a form is addressed in our Privacy Policy.

09

Updates And Contact

We may update this policy when the website or its service providers change. The date at the top of the page shows the current version.

Questions about cookies or site storage may be sent to hello@sundayandcompany.co.

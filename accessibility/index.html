01

Our Commitment

Sunday & Company wants this website to be useful to as many people as possible. Accessibility is part of our ongoing design and maintenance work, and we review the experience as content and features change.

02

Accessibility Standard

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, WCAG 2.1 Level AA, where reasonably applicable. These guidelines address common barriers affecting people who use assistive technology, keyboards, magnification or alternative ways of perceiving content.

03

Measures We Take

The site uses semantic headings, descriptive labels, keyboard-accessible controls and visible focus states. We provide text alternatives for meaningful images and avoid using color as the only way to communicate required information.

Layouts are designed to respond across screen sizes, and text can be enlarged through browser settings. Forms include labels, required-field information and status messages intended to work with assistive technology.

04

Keyboard And Navigation

Interactive elements are intended to be reachable by keyboard in a logical order. Menus, dialogs, accordions, links and form controls are designed to support standard keyboard interaction. Page headings and landmarks help assistive technology users move through content.

05

Forms And Communication

If an online form creates a barrier, you may contact us directly by email. Please do not include sensitive personal information when describing an accessibility issue. We will work with you to provide the same information or a reasonable alternative way to communicate.

06

Third-Party Content

Some linked services or embedded tools are provided by third parties. We do not control every part of their accessibility. We encourage our providers to support accessible experiences and welcome notice when a third-party feature creates a barrier.

07

Known Limitations And Ongoing Work

Accessibility is an ongoing process, and some content or browser combinations may not perform as expected. We review reported issues, prioritize barriers affecting core navigation and forms, and make reasonable improvements as the website evolves.

08

Accessibility Feedback

To report an accessibility problem, email hello@sundayandcompany.co with the page address, a short description of the issue and the browser or assistive technology you were using if you are comfortable sharing it.

We will acknowledge the message and work toward a reasonable response. Requests are reviewed individually because the appropriate solution depends on the content and barrier involved.

Frequently Asked Questions

More detail about our services, pricing and what happens after you inquire.

We work with lifestyle brands through branding, website design and digital marketing. Branding covers the strategy and visual identity. Website design includes the structure, custom design and development of your site. Digital marketing covers the ongoing work that keeps your brand visible, including content planning, copywriting and campaign support.

You do not need to diagnose the project before reaching out. Tell us what is changing in the business, what currently feels unclear and what you want the work to support. We will review the context and recommend the service or combined scope that fits the project.

The starting price is the entry point for that service. Your final investment depends on the size of the project, the deliverables involved and the condition of what already exists. You will receive a defined scope and price before the work begins.

Yes. Branding and website design are often completed together, and digital marketing can follow once the foundation is ready. If your project crosses more than one service, we will organize it into a clear scope instead of asking you to piece separate offers together.

Share your business name, current website or social profile, the service you are considering, your preferred timing and the investment range you have planned. Include anything that would help us understand what is already working and where you need more clarity.

We review the information and the links you provide before responding. If the project fits our services, the next step is a conversation about the scope and what you need from the work. You will understand the recommended direction before making a commitment.

Sunday & Company works remotely across the DMV and North Carolina, with clients everywhere else. Every project runs the same way regardless of where you are: shared documents, scheduled reviews and clear written decisions, so distance never changes the quality of the work.

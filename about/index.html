About Sunday

Sunday & Co. was born in the DMV (D.C., Maryland and Virginia) and has roots in North Carolina. We are a marketing and design studio working with clients everywhere. We build brand identities and websites, then carry that same thinking into digital marketing so a business feels consistent wherever people find it. We work with lifestyle brands that have good ideas and need a clearer way to show up and connect with the right people.

The name Sunday comes from the feeling we want the work to carry. It should feel considered and worth coming back to. We built Sunday for business owners who want thoughtful work without the jargon or a process that keeps them at arm’s length. You should understand what we are making and why each choice belongs.

That is what A Seat At Our Table means to us. You stay close to the work, and we keep the process clear from the first conversation through launch. When the work goes live, your brand should feel easier to recognize and easier to trust. It should still feel like yours, just clearer than it did before.

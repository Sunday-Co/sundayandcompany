01

Scope Of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the Sunday & Company website, including project inquiries, contact messages and newsletter signups. It does not govern a client project after a separate written agreement is signed, or the practices of websites and services we do not control.

References to Sunday, we, us and our mean Sunday & Company Marketing LLC.

02

Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to provide through our forms. This may include your name, email address, phone number, business name, current website, social media profile, estimated budget, ideal start date, services of interest and the project details you submit.

When you join The Sunday Reservation, we collect your email address. Our hosting and form providers may also receive limited technical information needed to deliver the site and protect forms from misuse, such as browser information, device information, IP address and request timing.

03

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to review inquiries, respond to messages, recommend an appropriate service and manage follow-up communication. Newsletter information is used to send the updates you requested and maintain subscription records.

We may also use limited technical information to operate the website, prevent spam, diagnose problems and understand how the site performs. We do not sell or rent personal information.

04

Forms And Service Providers

Website forms are processed through Web3Forms and delivered to Sunday & Company by email. Web3Forms and our website hosting provider process information on our behalf according to their own terms and privacy practices.

Please do not submit payment card details, government identification numbers, health information or other sensitive personal information through a general inquiry or contact form.

05

When Information Is Shared

We may share information with service providers that support website hosting, form delivery, email, security and professional operations. They receive only the information reasonably needed to perform their work.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to respond to a valid legal request, to protect the rights and safety of Sunday or others, or in connection with a sale or transfer of business assets subject to applicable law.

06

Cookies And Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies, session storage and similar technologies needed for basic operation. For example, session storage remembers when the newsletter invitation has been dismissed so it does not repeatedly interrupt the same browsing session.

We do not currently use advertising cookies. If optional analytics or advertising technology is added, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated before that technology is used.

07

Data Retention

We keep inquiry and contact information for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain business records, prepare a proposal or manage a client relationship. Newsletter information is kept until you unsubscribe or request deletion, subject to records we may need to retain for legal or compliance purposes.

Retention periods vary according to the nature of the information and why it was collected. Information that is no longer needed may be deleted or de-identified.

08

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Access is limited to people and providers who need the information for legitimate business purposes.

No online service or method of transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for choosing what information to include in open text fields and for using a secure device and connection when submitting a form.

09

Your Choices And Rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information we maintain about you, subject to identity verification and any legal exceptions. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply.

You may unsubscribe from marketing email through the link provided in a message or by contacting us. We will not discriminate against you for making a privacy request permitted by law.

10

Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for businesses and a general adult audience. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such information was submitted, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

11

Third-Party Links

The website may link to social platforms or other third-party websites. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Sunday & Company is not responsible for their content, privacy practices or security.

12

Policy Changes And Contact

We may update this policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. The effective and last-updated dates at the top of this page identify the current version.

Privacy requests and security concerns may be sent to hello@sundayandcompany.co. Please include enough detail for us to understand and respond to your request.

Questions

Contact Sunday & Company

Questions about this page can be sent to hello@sundayandcompany.co. We aim to respond to privacy and data requests within 30 days.
